The **GSTR-9 Annual Return** is a consolidated summary of all GST transactions for a financial year. Filing it correctly is critical — errors here can invite scrutiny.

## Who Must File GSTR-9?

Every regular GST-registered taxpayer with annual aggregate turnover **above ₹2 crore** must file GSTR-9.

Taxpayers below ₹2 crore have the option to file — and it is advisable to do so for a clean compliance record.

**Exempt from GSTR-9:**
- Composition scheme taxpayers (they file GSTR-9A)
- Input service distributors
- Non-resident taxable persons
- Casual taxable persons

## Due Date

GSTR-9 for FY 2023-24 is due by **31 December 2024**.

Late fee: ₹200 per day (₹100 CGST + ₹100 SGST), subject to a maximum of 0.25% of turnover.

## Key Tables in GSTR-9 Explained

### Table 4 — Outward Supplies
Reconcile total outward supplies declared in monthly GSTR-1 returns. Any differences must be explained.

### Table 6 — Input Tax Credit (ITC)
This is where most errors occur. You must reconcile ITC claimed in GSTR-3B vs ITC available in GSTR-2B.

### Table 8 — ITC Reconciliation
Compare ITC as per GSTR-2A/2B with what was actually claimed. Excess claimed ITC must be reversed with interest at **24% per annum**.

<Callout type="warning">
ITC reversal with interest is the most common compliance cost in GSTR-9. Run the reconciliation early — before the deadline — so you have time to take corrective action.
</Callout>

### Table 17 & 18 — HSN Summary
Outward and inward supply summary by HSN code. Required for taxpayers with turnover above ₹1.5 crore.

## GSTR-9C — Reconciliation Statement

Taxpayers with turnover **above ₹5 crore** must also file **GSTR-9C**, a self-certified reconciliation statement between audited financial statements and the GST returns.

From FY 2021-22, GSTR-9C is self-certified by the taxpayer (previously it required CA certification).

## Practical Reconciliation Checklist

- [ ] Match turnover in GSTR-1 vs books of accounts
- [ ] Verify tax paid in GSTR-3B matches GSTR-1 liability
- [ ] Reconcile ITC in GSTR-3B vs GSTR-2B
- [ ] Check RCM (Reverse Charge Mechanism) liability declared and paid
- [ ] Verify all amendments and credit/debit notes are included
- [ ] Confirm e-way bill and e-invoice data consistency

## Most Common Errors and How to Fix Them

1. **Turnover mismatch** — Often due to advances received or branch transfers; adjust in Table 5 with valid reasons
2. **ITC excess claimed** — Reverse in GSTR-3B for remaining months with DRC-03 payment
3. **Missed B2B invoices** — Can be included in Table 10/11 (amendments of previous FY)
