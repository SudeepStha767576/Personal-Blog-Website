Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) can feel overwhelming, but once you understand the process it becomes straightforward. This guide covers everything a salaried employee needs.

## Which ITR Form Should You Use?

For most salaried individuals with income from salary, one house property, and interest, **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** is the correct form.

Use **ITR-2** if you have:
- Capital gains income
- Income from more than one house property
- Foreign income or assets

## Key Deadlines for AY 2024-25

| Filing Type | Due Date |
|-------------|----------|
| Non-audit cases (individuals) | 31 July 2024 |
| Audit cases | 31 October 2024 |
| Belated return | 31 December 2024 |

> **Tip:** File before 31 July to avoid a late fee of ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if total income ≤ ₹5 lakh).

## Documents You Need

1. **Form 16** — issued by your employer, covers salary and TDS
2. **Form 26AS / AIS** — verify TDS credits at incometax.gov.in
3. **Bank interest certificates** — for savings and FD interest
4. **Investment proofs** — 80C, 80D, 80G receipts

## Popular Deductions Under Old Regime

### Section 80C (up to ₹1,50,000)
- PPF, ELSS mutual funds, NSC, life insurance premium
- Principal repayment of home loan
- Children's tuition fees

### Section 80D — Health Insurance
- ₹25,000 for self/family
- Additional ₹25,000 for parents (₹50,000 if senior citizen)

### Section 24(b) — Home Loan Interest
Up to ₹2,00,000 deduction on interest paid on a self-occupied property.

## New Tax Regime vs Old Regime

The **New Tax Regime** (default from FY 2023-24) offers lower slab rates but most deductions are not available.

**Choose New Regime if:** Your deductions under 80C, 80D, HRA etc. are low.
**Stick with Old Regime if:** Your total deductions exceed ₹3–4 lakh.

<Callout type="info">
Use the tax calculator on incometax.gov.in to compare both regimes with your actual numbers before deciding.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not reporting interest income** — banks report to the Income Tax Department; mismatches trigger notices
- **Missing Form 26AS verification** — always cross-check TDS credits
- **Skipping exempt income** — HRA exemption, LTA must be correctly claimed
- **Ignoring capital gains** on mutual fund redemptions or stock sales

## How to File Online

1. Login at [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Go to **e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return**
3. Select AY 2024-25 and ITR-1
4. Pre-filled data will appear — verify and correct each section
5. Compute tax, pay if due, then e-verify using Aadhaar OTP
