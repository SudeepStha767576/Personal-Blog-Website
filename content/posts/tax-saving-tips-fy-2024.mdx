Tax planning is not tax evasion — it is your legal right. The Income Tax Act offers numerous provisions that reduce your tax liability if used correctly and on time.

## 1. Max Out Section 80C — ₹1,50,000 Deduction

The most widely used deduction. Options include:

- **ELSS Mutual Funds** — 3-year lock-in, market-linked returns (best for wealth creation)
- **PPF** — 15-year tenure, tax-free maturity, EEE status
- **NPS Tier-I** — Additional ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B) over and above 80C limit
- **Life Insurance Premium**
- **Home Loan Principal Repayment**

<Callout type="info">
NPS gives you a **total deduction of ₹2,00,000** (₹1,50,000 under 80C + ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)) — making it the single best tax-saving instrument for someone without a home loan.
</Callout>

## 2. Health Insurance — Section 80D

Don't buy health insurance just for the deduction — buy it because you need it. The tax benefit is a bonus.

| Coverage | Deduction |
|----------|-----------|
| Self + family (below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (60+) | ₹50,000 |
| **Maximum possible** | **₹1,00,000** |

## 3. Home Loan — Double Benefit

A home loan gives deductions on both principal (80C) and interest (24b):
- Principal: up to ₹1,50,000 within 80C limit
- Interest: up to ₹2,00,000 for self-occupied property
- Let-out property: **unlimited** interest deduction (against rental income)

## 4. HRA Exemption — Salaried Employees

If you live in a rented home, claim **House Rent Allowance (HRA)** exemption. The exempt amount is the **minimum** of:
- Actual HRA received
- Rent paid minus 10% of salary
- 50% of salary (metro) / 40% (non-metro)

> Keep rent receipts and ensure landlord's PAN is quoted for rent above ₹1 lakh/year.

## 5. Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)

Claim LTA for domestic travel costs (air/rail/road) for you and your family. Available twice in a block of four calendar years. The current block is **2022–2025**.

## 6. Form 12BB — Submit Investment Proofs to Employer

Submit your investment declarations and actual proofs to your employer before March so they reduce TDS correctly. Missing this means you pay excess TDS and wait for refund.

## 7. Capital Gains Harvesting

If you have equity investments with unrealised gains, consider **tax harvesting**:
- Sell and rebuy before March 31
- Book ₹1,00,000 of LTCG tax-free each year (equity LTCG exemption)
- Resets your cost basis

## 8. Donate to Save Tax — Section 80G

Donations to approved funds (PM Relief Fund, PMNRF) qualify for **100% deduction**. Most other registered NGOs qualify for 50% deduction.

Always verify the NGO's 80G registration on the Income Tax portal before donating.

## 9. Interest on Education Loan — Section 80E

If you or your spouse/children took an education loan, **100% of the interest paid** is deductible for up to 8 years. No upper limit.

## 10. Consider a Family HUF

A **Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)** is a separate tax entity that gets its own basic exemption limit (₹3 lakh under new regime). If you have ancestral property or family income, an HUF can legally split income and reduce overall family tax.

<Callout type="warning">
HUF planning must be set up with proper documentation. Consult a CA before forming one — incorrect setup can create complications later.
</Callout>
